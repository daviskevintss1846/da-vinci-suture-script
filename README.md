## What the Simulator Provides

Da Vinci Suture Training Simulator is a web medical simulation game designed for robotic-assisted suturing practice. Its three exercises move from grape suturing to human skin work and intestinal anastomosis, offering progressively different procedural settings.

Instrument handling works through mouse dragging or mobile touch input, with movement divided into clutch-like segments. The simulator also includes selectable motion ratios, tremor reduction, suture management, and changing bleeding and breathing effects that influence the practice sessions.

## Capabilities

- Practice grape suturing, human skin suturing, and intestinal anastomosis across three levels
- Select 1:1, 3:1, or 5:1 motion scaling
- Reduce the effect of hand tremor while operating the instruments
- Control instruments with mouse dragging or touch gestures on mobile devices
- Use segmented, clutch-style instrument movement
- Encounter simulated bleeding and breathing changes during exercises
- Tie sutures and complete the associated procedure
- Receive an S-to-F result and maintain surgical records
- Run the utility from a self-contained HTML file without external dependencies
